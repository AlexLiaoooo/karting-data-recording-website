# Map attributions

## PF International owner-driver schematic

`public/maps/pfi-international-owner-driver.svg` is a simplified schematic generated for this project from OpenStreetMap raceway geometry downloaded from the PF International area. It is a derived work, not an official PF International promotional map.

The underlying OpenStreetMap data is © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and is available under the [Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The website displays this attribution next to the map.

The official circuit page describes the current full circuit as 1,382 m: [PF International — The Circuit](https://kartpfi.com/the-circuit). Its official artwork is not bundled here because PF International's company information page reserves rights to its content: [Company Information](https://kartpfi.com/legal/company-information).

## Buckmore Park circuit schematic

`public/maps/buckmore-park.svg` is generated by `scripts/build-buckmore-map.mjs` from
`scripts/data/buckmore-park-osm.json`, an Overpass extract retrieved on 2026-08-30. It is a derived
work, not official Buckmore Park artwork.

The underlying data is © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), credited in the file and in the
app beneath the map.

This is the least reconstructed of the three. OpenStreetMap maps the circuit as a single closed way
(92128535) tagged `oneway=yes`, so the lap is that way and the clockwise direction is the order of
its nodes; neither was chosen. The lap begins at the pit exit, the nearest thing the source records
to a start line. The extract's other ways — five cut-throughs for the shorter layouts, two access
roads and two short pit lane stubs — are not drawn.

The measured centreline is 854 m against the 929 m the circuit publishes, and twelve corners are
detected, which is the number it publishes.

## Kart Silverstone Grand Prix circuit schematic

`public/maps/silverstone-grand-prix.svg` is generated by `scripts/build-silverstone-map.mjs` from
`scripts/data/silverstone-osm.json`, an Overpass extract retrieved on 2026-08-28 covering the
eleven `highway=raceway` `sport=karting` ways at Silverstone. It is a derived work, not official
Kart Silverstone artwork; their own layout diagrams are not bundled here.

The underlying data is © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), credited in the file and in the
app beneath the map.

Unlike the other two circuits, this one is a **reconstruction**. OpenStreetMap has no
`type=circuit` relation and no `oneway` tagging at Silverstone, so neither the lap nor the racing
direction is recorded; the network contains 154 distinct closed laps. The lap drawn is the one
whose measured centreline (1,378 m) is nearest the 1,377 m the operator publishes for the Grand
Prix layout, and which scored best when candidates were aligned against the operator's diagram —
but six candidates lie within 2% of that length, so the match is not established. The circuit
publishes 18 corners for this layout; the same detector that labels the other two circuits finds
15 here, which is a further sign the reconstruction is approximate.

The **anti-clockwise direction is not from OpenStreetMap either** — it was supplied by the owner,
who drives there, and the generator orients the lap to it before numbering the corners. It is
recorded here rather than in the extract so that its source is not mistaken for the survey.

No start/finish line is drawn, because nothing records one: the lap begins at its northernmost
point purely so the numbering is stable between runs. The track note in the app says the layout is
reconstructed, and that the corners run in the circuit's order but start where the app counts from
rather than necessarily where the circuit does.

## Whilton Mill International circuit schematic

`public/maps/whilton-mill-international.svg` is a schematic generated for this project by
`scripts/build-whilton-maps.mjs` from OpenStreetMap raceway geometry. It is a derived work, not
official Whilton Mill artwork.

The source geometry is committed at `scripts/data/whilton-mill-osm.json`, retrieved from the
Overpass API on 2026-08-19. It is kept as retrieved, so it still covers all three circuit
relations Whilton Mill has mapped — International (16338535), National (16338536) and Indy
(16338537) — plus the closed way for the Mill circuit (149913876) and the two pit lanes, even
though only the International circuit is shipped. The underlying data is
© [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and available under the
[Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
Every generated file carries the attribution in its own caption, and the website shows it beneath
the map.

The lap length printed on the map, 1,040 m, is the measured centreline of that geometry. It is
labelled "centreline" because it is not the operator's published distance. Whilton Mill's karting
page quotes a "450m Mill Circuit" and a "960m National Circuit", and it publishes no figure for
the International circuit. Measuring the same way gave 441 m for the Mill circuit, within 2% of
their figure, but 845 m for the National, well short of theirs — which suggests their distances
are measured differently or refer to different configurations. No official figure is reproduced
here as if it were derived from this data.
